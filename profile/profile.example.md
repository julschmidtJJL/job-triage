# Fit profile (example)

The real profile lives in `profile/profile.md` (gitignored) and, on GitHub Actions,
in the `FIT_PROFILE` secret. Claude scores every posting against it, so write it
for a reader who knows nothing about you. Two to three pages is plenty.

## What I have built and shipped
- Concrete things, with evidence: what it does, who used it, what it runs on.

## How I work / strengths
- What people come to you for. What you do better than the job title suggests.

## Situations that fit
- The company's situation matters more than its industry. For example: a company
  without AI in use that wants its first person to build with it, close to management.

## Role shapes I'm looking for
- e.g. forward-deployed / AI solutions engineer, internal AI lead, data engineer.

## Anti-signals
- Situations or roles to score down, and why.

## Hard constraints
- Location, languages, working model, anything that rules a posting out.

## Already applied to / rejected by
- Company — role — month — outcome.

## Open items (fill in)
- Salary floor, part-time yes/no, seniority ceiling.
